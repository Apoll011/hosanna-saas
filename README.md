# Hosanna

> **Plan your worship. Organize your music. Serve with confidence.**

Hosanna is a worship planning platform designed to help churches organize their music library, prepare services, and give every musician quick access to the songs they need.

Whether you're leading worship, playing an instrument, singing, or preparing a service, Hosanna keeps your music and service plans together in one place. Instead of juggling folders, messaging apps, and printed chord sheets, your entire worship team works from a single, organized library.

Built around the way churches actually prepare for worship, Hosanna focuses on being simple, fast, and distraction-free.

---

# Why Hosanna?

Many churches rely on a mix of paper songbooks, cloud storage, messaging apps, and expensive or overly complicated software to prepare worship services. These solutions often make it difficult to organize music, keep everyone using the latest version of a song, or quickly prepare a service.

Hosanna was created to solve these problems.

It provides a clean, focused environment where churches can:

- Build and organize a complete song library.
- Prepare worship services in advance.
- Give every musician access to the same songs.
- Make personal adjustments without affecting anyone else.
- Keep music available even when there is no internet connection.

The goal is simple: spend less time managing files and more time preparing for worship.

---

# Studio and Mobile App

Hosanna consists of two applications that work together.

## Studio

The Studio is where worship leaders and church staff manage everything.

From here you can:

- Create and organize your song library.
- Import and export songs.
- Edit lyrics and chords.
- Organize songs into folders.
- Create worship services.
- Arrange the order of songs.
- Add notes for the entire worship team.
- Schedule services with titles and dates.

The Studio is designed for planning and administration, making it easy to manage large collections of music.

---

## Mobile App

The mobile app is designed for musicians during rehearsals and live services.

It allows each musician to:

- View the planned service.
- Read songs clearly on any device.
- Transpose songs into a comfortable key.
- Hide or show chords independently.
- Adjust font size for better readability.
- Keep the screen awake during worship.
- Add personal notes.

Each musician can customize their own viewing experience without changing it for anyone else.

---

# Organize Your Music

Hosanna uses a familiar folder-based library, making it feel similar to organizing files on your computer.

Songs can be searched by:

- Title
- Artist
- Lyrics
- Musical key
- Tags
- Song number

Songs are stored using the ChordPro format, making them easy to maintain and portable between compatible applications.

---

# Plan Every Service

Creating a service takes only a few moments.

Simply create a new service, choose its title and date, then begin adding songs from your library.

You can:

- Arrange songs in any order.
- Add notes for individual songs.
- Add general notes for the entire service.
- Prepare everything before rehearsal.

Everyone viewing the service sees the same planned order, helping the entire team stay together.

---

# Built for Live Worship

During worship, Hosanna stays focused on what matters.

Musicians can move through the service at their own pace while keeping their own display preferences.

Because every musician is different, each device can independently:

- Transpose songs.
- Change text size.
- Show or hide chords.

This flexibility allows guitarists, pianists, vocalists, and other musicians to work comfortably without affecting the rest of the team.

---

# Offline Support

The mobile app keeps your music available even without an internet connection, allowing musicians to continue using previously synchronized songs during rehearsals or services.

When an internet connection is available, updates can be synchronized with the church's library.

---

# Built for Churches

Each church has its own independent music library and service plans.

A church may organize multiple worship teams while keeping everything within the same shared library.

This makes Hosanna suitable for churches of all sizes, from small congregations to large ministries with multiple worship teams.

---

# Import and Export

Hosanna supports importing and exporting songs using the ChordPro format, making it easy to migrate an existing library or keep backups of your music.

---

# PDF Export

Services can be exported as printable PDF documents from the mobile app.

The exported document includes:

- Service order
- Chords and lyrics for every song

Studio PDF generation is planned for a future release.
