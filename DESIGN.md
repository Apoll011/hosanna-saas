# Design

## Platform

web

## Color

The opening of every public page is a blue field: deep sky `#075985` into `#0c4a6e`, with a lighter blue wash. Five curved staff lines sit on that field in white at low opacity. That band is the header and the page mast together.

Below the mast the ground is cool paper `#f3f5f7`. Ink is `#1a2330`. Muted text is `#3d4c5c`. The logo blue `#16689f` is the action on paper (buttons, links, the current line in a list). On the blue mast the action is white. Borders are `#d3dbe3`. Success `#1c6b43`, warning `#8a5a12`, error `#9b2c2c`.

## Type

Display: Petrona Variable, slight negative tracking, for titles, prices, and quotes.
Text and UI: Source Sans 3 Variable.
Both are self-hosted and cover Portuguese, English, and Spanish.

The hero title is semibold and tops out near 4rem. Section titles are medium and top out near 2.35rem. Body is 1rem / 1.6. Prices use tabular numerals.

## Shape and elevation

Controls use an 8px radius. Screenshot frames use 12px. The logo sits on a white tile in the blue header so the blue mark stays visible.

One shadow: the product devices, a soft drop shadow. Lists and sections separate with hairline rules, not cards.

## Header

The nav is transparent over the blue mast, then becomes solid `#075985` with the same staff lines once the page scrolls. Type in the header is white. The trial button is white with blue text.
